# Enterprise-Wide Continuous Improvement Intelligence Platform using Six Sigma Master Black Belt

## Overview

**EnterpriseSigma** is a full-stack enterprise continuous-improvement management platform designed around **Six Sigma Master Black Belt principles**.

The platform moves beyond individual process-improvement projects and provides an enterprise-level view of improvement initiatives, DMAIC execution, financial benefits, project risks, mitigation activities, and cross-functional improvement performance.

It is designed to demonstrate how a Master Black Belt can establish governance across multiple Six Sigma projects and connect project-level improvements with broader organizational objectives.

The platform supports:

- Enterprise improvement portfolio management
- Six Sigma belt deployment
- DMAIC action tracking
- Defect-rate improvement analysis
- Financial savings tracking
- Enterprise risk management
- Risk scoring
- Mitigation planning
- Department-level analytics
- Belt-level portfolio analytics
- Executive improvement dashboards

---

# Project Objectives

The primary objectives of EnterpriseSigma are to:

- Manage multiple continuous-improvement initiatives.
- Track projects across different organizational departments.
- Monitor Six Sigma belt deployment.
- Track baseline, target, and actual defect performance.
- Manage improvement actions across DMAIC phases.
- Monitor project status and priorities.
- Estimate financial improvement value.
- Identify and prioritize project risks.
- Track risk mitigation activities.
- Analyze improvement performance across departments.
- Provide enterprise-level improvement visibility.
- Support structured continuous-improvement governance.
